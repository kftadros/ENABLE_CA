Making city services more accessible for every family.

Five SoCal community college students from different majors, setting out to audit Irvine's city services, interview families, propose specific fixes, and build them. We are just getting started.

Report a Barrier Partner With Us

What ENABLE Stands For

  • E – Engineering

    Designing practical, feasible improvements to city services.

  • N – Navigation

    Making information, spaces, and services easier to find and use.

  • A – Advocacy

    Bringing community needs to the city and championing real solutions.

  • B – Belonging

    Building a more welcoming, inclusive Irvine for all families.

  • L – Language

    Removing communication barriers through clear, multilingual, and culturally thoughtful resources.

  • E – Equity

    Ensuring Irvine's services work for people of all abilities, backgrounds, and life circumstances.

How We Work

A community-centered process that turns real experiences into real, built solutions.

  1. 1. Audit

    Review city services, spaces, and digital tools to find barriers.

  2. 2. Listen

    Interview families and community members to understand real needs.

  3. 3. Propose

    Turn insights into specific, actionable fixes for the city.

  4. 4. Build

    Develop and launch those fixes, from clearer resources to working tools.

Where We're Starting

  • City Websites & Forms

  • Parks & Programs
