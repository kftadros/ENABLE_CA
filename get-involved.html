Quick answers

How quickly will I hear back?

Usually within 72 hours, if you left contact details.

How is my information used?

Only to understand and follow up on the barrier you reported. Reports are read by the ENABLE team. We may describe the barrier itself in our findings or in proposals to the city, but never with your name or contact details unless you say yes first. To have your report deleted, email enableca.org@gmail.com.

Can I report anonymously?

Yes. Name and email are optional.

We use your information only to follow up on your report, and we don't share your name or contact details without your permission. Read our privacy page.
