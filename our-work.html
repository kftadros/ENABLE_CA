Our work

Our plan: audit city services, listen to families, propose fixes, and build solutions. We are at the start of that process, and this page will fill in as the work happens.

How we work

Four steps, starting with what residents tell us.

  1. 1. Audit

    Check city buildings, parks, websites and forms for barriers.

  2. 2. Listen

    Interview families and community members to understand real needs.

  3. 3. Propose

    Write up specific fixes and bring them to the city.

  4. 4. Build

    Build the fixes we can make ourselves, like plain-language guides or web tools.

Planned focus areas

The areas we plan to examine first.

  • Sidewalks and curb ramps

    Checking sidewalks and curb ramps for barriers to getting around.

  • City websites and forms

    Testing city web pages and forms against WCAG 2.1 AA.

  • Parks and programs

    Looking at access to parks, recreation programs, and community activities.

  • Language access

    Reviewing whether families can find and understand city services in their language.
