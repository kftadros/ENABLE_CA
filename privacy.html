Privacy

What this site collects and what we do with it, in plain language.

Last updated November 14, 2026

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through the barrier report form:

  • where the barrier is, what kind it is, and your description of it
  • a photo, if you upload one
  • your name, email address and how you would like us to follow up, if you give them

Name and email are optional. You can report a barrier without them.

How we use it

To understand the barrier you reported, follow up with you if you asked us to, and decide what ENABLE looks at next.

We may describe the barrier itself in our findings or in proposals to the city. We never include your name or contact details unless you say yes first, and we will not publish a photo you sent without your permission.

Who sees it

Reports are read by the ENABLE team. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your report

Email enableca.org@gmail.com and we will show you what we have, correct it, or delete it.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, we will update the date at the top. Questions about any of this can go to enableca.org@gmail.com.

enableca.org

A student-led project in Irvine, CA, working to make city services easier to use for people with disabilities and their families. We are just getting started.
